Third Law: The Law of Action and Reaction

"For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction."

This law explains why when you push against a wall, the wall pushes back with an equal force. Similarly, when a rocket expels gas downward, the gas pushes the rocket upward with an equal force.

Key Idea: Forces always come in pairs—equal in magnitude and opposite in direction.

Real-World Applications

Newton's Third Law is essential in countless real-world scenarios:

• Rockets use this principle to propel themselves by expelling gas downward, which creates an upward thrust.

• Jet engines work similarly, expelling air backward to move the plane forward.

• When you walk, your foot pushes backward on the ground, and the ground pushes you forward.

• Even swimming relies on this law—you push water backward, and the water pushes you forward.
